About

Every pattern begins somewhere small.

Soph Rushton is an artist working between Amsterdam and London, exploring how patterns emerge from living systems. Her work studies growth, rhythm, and transformation through carefully structured visual compositions.

Drawing from natural logic and historical textile languages, Soph builds pieces that hold both emotion and order. The result is artwork that feels timeless, tactile, and deeply personal.

Her studio creates bespoke commissions for individuals and collaborators looking for pattern work with meaning: designs that do more than decorate, and instead carry memory, identity, and connection.
