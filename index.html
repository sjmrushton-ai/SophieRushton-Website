A living cartography of pattern.

Soph Rushton is an artist exploring the space between nature, memory, and indigenous textile cosmologies. Her work traces how patterns grow, repeat, branch, and transform — becoming visual systems that feel alive.

Inspired by biological forms, Amazonian kene, Andean tocapu, mapping, and symbolic language, Soph creates patterns that hold identity, ecology, rhythm, and story. Her pieces are not decorative motifs, but personal visual languages: structured, intuitive, and quietly sentient.

Working across ink, print, embroidery, and gold work, Soph builds bespoke compositions from initials, symbols, organic forms, and repeated marks. Each work becomes a living map — part artwork, part archive, part pattern system.

A living cartography of pattern merging nature and indigenous textile cosmologies into a sentient system.
